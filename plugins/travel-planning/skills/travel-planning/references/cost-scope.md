# Structured cost scope

Transport `cost` and attraction cost-item `price` may use `{ "amount": 200, "currency": "AUD", "basis": "group", "traveler_count": 2, "taxes": "included" }`. Use an explicit uppercase three-letter currency code and a basis of `per_person`, `per_adult`, `group`, `per_vehicle`, `per_item`, or `per_room_per_night`. Optional `display` preserves the provider's wording. `taxes` is `included`, `excluded`, or `unknown`; omission does not imply taxes are included. Finite nonnegative amounts, including zero and numeric strings, are supported up to 1,000 integer/fractional digits. Counts are positive integers up to 1,000,000.

Assembly retains the original transport object in `cost_quote` and cost-item price in `price_evidence`; existing CNY fields remain compatible. A cost item's explicit `quantity` multiplies its pricing unit, never an inferred traveler count. Derived subtotals are labeled planning estimates, while supplied subtotal text remains intact. Missing quantity stays unknown. Currency amounts remain separate; no exchange rate, conversion, or cross-currency grand total is invented.

Set `trip.traveler_count` to the whole party size when available. Only unambiguous legacy descriptions such as `2 位成人` or `2 adults` are interpreted. Quote `traveler_count` also refers to the whole party covered, not vehicle capacity or an adult/child subgroup. A selected transport or event quote for another party size blocks audit; unknown trip size warns, and unselected transport candidates do not block. The legacy `amount_yuan_for_4` explicitly covers four travelers. Cost items that are mutually exclusive may share an `alternative_group`; at most one item in that group may be `baseline` within an event.
